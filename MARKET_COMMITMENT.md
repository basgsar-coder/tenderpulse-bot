# MARKET_COMMITMENT.md: Agent-Task Marketplace Alignment

## 1. Target Buyer Persona
**The target buyer is an enterprise data engineering team, compliance officer, or autonomous AI orchestrator that deposits pre-funded data extraction, document verification, and research bounties on the MercatAI B2B Agent Marketplace ([https://mercatai.eu](https://mercatai.eu)).**

---

## 2. Why This Buyer Uses the Chosen Channel (MercatAI)
Enterprise platforms and multi-agent workflows require structured, verified real-world data (such as regulatory compliance extractions, public tender specifications, and technical document synthesis) without maintaining hundreds of brittle, bespoke scrapers or employing human data entry teams. MercatAI provides these organizations with an automated, EU AI Act-compliant marketplace where they can post structured task specifications with escrowed budgets, set automated validation schemas, and receive guaranteed, cryptographic audit-trailed deliverables from specialized worker agents via standardized APIs.

---

## 3. Why This Buyer Pays €15.00 to €35.00 (~$16.50 to $38.50 USD) Per Task
Hiring a junior analyst or human data contractor to manually research, extract, clean, and verify complex technical or regulatory documentation costs $35.00 to $70.00 per hour and introduces human error and multi-day delivery delays. By posting the task to MercatAI at €15–€35 per completed batch, the buyer cuts data acquisition costs by 60% while reducing delivery latency from days to under two minutes. Because tasks are pre-funded into platform escrow, payment is mathematically guaranteed upon automated schema validation.

---

## 4. What We Are Explicitly NOT Building (Anti-Scope)
1. **We are NOT building a retail human SaaS or consumer dashboard:** We are not building Stripe checkout landing pages aimed at convincing individual human freelancers to enter credit cards.
2. **We are NOT building a federal teaming or procurement sales agency:** We reject all pre-award bid matchmaking and cold outreach to prime contractors. Pre-award bids are proprietary secrets, and cold outreach violates autonomous execution directives.
3. **We are strictly building an autonomous machine-to-machine task worker agent:** Our product is a certified, RFC-compliant worker node that registers on MercatAI via `mercatai-agent`, polls for pre-funded data extraction and compliance tasks, executes them autonomously, and collects programmatic payouts directly into our ledger.
